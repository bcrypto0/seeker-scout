Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Seeker Scout ("the app") is built to respect your privacy by design. This policy explains what the app does — and, more importantly, what it does not do.

What we collect

Nothing. Seeker Scout has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.

How the app works with data

  • App catalog: the app downloads a public catalog file (app names, ratings, categories) from our server. This request contains no personal data.
  • Wallet connection (optional): if you choose to connect your wallet, the connection happens on your device through the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter. Your keys never leave your wallet, and we never see or store them.
  • Genesis Token verification (optional): to verify Seeker ownership, the app queries the public Solana blockchain for token accounts associated with your wallet address. This read-only lookup is sent to a public RPC endpoint. Wallet addresses are public information on the blockchain; the app does not store your address or send it to our servers.

Data stored on your device

Any preferences or connection state live only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare (catalog and website hosting) — standard server logs may include IP addresses, retained briefly for security.
  • Public Solana RPC endpoints (blockchain reads only).

Children

The app does not target children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted at this URL with a new date.
