Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Seeker Scout ("the app") works without an account, and most of it sends nothing about you. This policy lists exactly what the app sends to our server, what we keep, and why.

What we collect

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell the data below, and we share it only with the service providers named under Third-party services. Our server runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database).

  • App-open counter: each launch sends an anonymous ping with the app's build number. Once a day it also says how many days ago the app was installed, as a range (for example "2-3" or "8-14"), and whether this is the app's first launch. It carries no account, wallet address, device identifier or install date. We use it to count daily opens and to see how long installs are kept.
  • Owners' Lounge (optional): verifying your Seeker Genesis Token and claiming a Lounge number sends your wallet address, the Genesis Token's address and a message signed by your wallet. We store the wallet address, the token address, your Lounge number and the claim time.
  • Members' chat (optional): messages you post are stored with your Lounge number and wallet address, and shown to other members with your Lounge number. Reactions are stored with your Lounge number. Reports are stored with the reporting wallet address.
  • Scout Daily games (optional): your guesses, picks and scores are stored with your Lounge number and wallet address. The public leaderboard shows Lounge numbers and scores, not wallet addresses.
  • Scout Alpha (optional, paid): subscribing sends your wallet address, a signed message and the payment's transaction signature. We store the wallet address, the paid-until date and the transaction signatures, to check each payment is used once.
  • Scout Vouch (optional): vouching for an app sends your wallet address, your Genesis Token's address, a message signed by your wallet, the app's package id, your verdict (works or broken), the tags you picked, your note and the time you signed. We store these with the signature, the time we received the vouch, the weight it counts with, the amount of staked SKR that weight was worked out from (empty if your stake could not be read) and the time of that read. A later vouch from the same wallet can update the weight and staked amount stored on its earlier vouches. We also keep your wallet address with the time of your last vouch, to allow one vouch every 10 seconds. Notes cannot contain links. Your verdict counts in each app's public totals. These totals count Genesis Tokens and show no one's weight or stake. Weights are used to rank apps, so where an app with few vouches ranks can hint at how much SKR its vouchers stake. A note is shown publicly on the app's page with your verdict, your tags, the time of your vouch and your Lounge number if you claimed one, without any weight or stake. After you vouch, the app shows you your own weight and staked SKR. Anyone who knows your Genesis Token's address can look up which apps it vouched for and its verdict on each. While your wallet is connected, opening an app page sends your Genesis Token's address to our server so the app can show your own vouch.
  • SKR stake (with Scout Vouch): each time you vouch, our server reads the SKR your wallet has staked in Solana Mobile's public staking program, through our RPC provider, or reuses a read of your wallet made in the last 60 seconds. Staked SKR raises your voice from 1.00x up to 4.00x; SKR waiting to be unstaked does not count. If your wallet vouches with more than one Seeker Genesis Token, its stake is shared equally between them. A vouch whose stake read fails counts 1.00x. A vouch made before we started reading stakes counts 1.00x until you vouch again and your stake is read. For each wallet we keep one stake record, replaced at each read: your wallet address, whether a stake was found, the amount staked, any amount waiting to be unstaked and when that unstake started, the staking program's cooldown period and share price at that time, the weight before sharing, the blockchain slot that was read and the time of the read. A read that fails is not kept in this record. We also keep your wallet address with the address of its stake account in the staking program, so it does not have to be worked out again.
  • Blockchain checks: to share them fairly, our server counts them per minute: by Genesis Token for a wallet and token it has checked before, otherwise by your network address (an IPv6 address counts by its first half). Each counter is deleted at the first check that arrives more than two minutes after its minute.
  • App catalog, banners, rewards lists, vouch totals and feature switches: the app downloads public data from our server. These requests contain no personal data.

Wallet addresses and transaction signatures are public on the Solana blockchain.

Your wallet

  • Wallet connection (optional): the connection happens on your device through the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter. Your keys stay in your wallet; we do not see or store them.
  • Genesis Token verification (optional): the app asks a public Solana RPC endpoint which tokens your wallet address holds. This is a read-only blockchain lookup.

Data stored on your device

Your watchlist, preferences, the Lounge sign-in token (valid for about a day), the date the open counter last sent its daily install range, your last vouch for each app (the server's answer to it: verdict, tags, note, weight, staked SKR and Lounge number, stored with your Genesis Token's address so the app can show them back to you) and the last copy of the feature switches live only on your device, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep Lounge, chat, game, vouch, SKR stake and Alpha records while those features run. You can ask us to delete the records linked to your wallet address by writing to the contact address below from a channel where you can show you control that wallet.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare (website, catalog, Lounge server and database): standard server logs may include IP addresses, retained briefly for security.
  • Solana RPC providers (blockchain reads by the app, and by our server to check that a wallet holds its Genesis Token and to read the SKR it has staked; the provider sees these staking account lookups).
  • Your wallet app, through the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (signing only when you approve).

Children

The app does not target children.

Changes
